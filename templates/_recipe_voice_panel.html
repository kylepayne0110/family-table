Tell me the recipe

Take your time. I'll fill in the form while you talk and ask about anything missing when you're done.

Your voice is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition (on iPhone, Apple's dictation service; in Chrome, Google's). Family Table receives only the text and never stores audio. Typing in the form works at the same time. Closing this, or saying “sounds good,” never saves: review the recipe and press Save.
